Any character can zap a wand, but all wands have a specific number of charges. When all charges have been used, the wand will either do nothing or function one last time and then turn to dust (1/121 chance) – this is known as wresting\footnotemark[20]\myhref{20}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Wresting} a last charge out of the wand. Y = number of times it has been recharged. Z = current number of charges. A current charge count of -{}1 is a special case indicating that the wand has been cancelled.


A wand\textquotesingle{}s effect is not known before it is identified: they only appear by description, such as \symbol{34}an ivory wand\symbol{34} (see §{\mbox{$~$}}Appearance\footnotemark[22]\mylref{22}{25}).


All wands have a weight of 7.


Wands (other than the wand of lightning\footnotemark[23]\myhref{23}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Wand_of_lightning}) have a 1 in 3 chance of exploding when subjected to shock\footnotemark[24]\myhref{24}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Shock} damage.\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{{$\text{[}$}1{$\text{]}$}}

\subsection{Direction, beams, and rays}
\label{4}
The type of wand denotes the behavior when it is zapped. Non-{}directional wands do not ask for a direction. Beam\footnotemark[80]\myhref{80}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Beam} wands ask for a direction, but do not show a visibly animated effect when zapped. Ray\footnotemark[81]\myhref{81}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Ray} wands produce a ray that is animated on the screen; if you are unblind when you zap the wand, you will also identify that type of wand. Rays may bounce off walls\footnotemark[82]\myhref{82}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Ray\#Bouncing_rays} or be reflected\footnotemark[83]\myhref{83}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Reflection}.


When a wand asks you for a direction, you can answer with any of the standard directions\footnotemark[84]\myhref{84}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Direction}, including the cardinal directions, up, down, or self. If you refuse to answer a direction, then the wand glows and fades, and the zap is lost.\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{{$\text{[}$}3{$\text{]}$}}


If a ray hits a wall orthogonally or at an internal corner it will bounce straight back. If it hits a wall diagonally or an external corner, there is a 5\% chance it will bounce straight back, otherwise it will change direction 90 degrees, with equal chances of being deflected left or right. Note that on diagonal wall bounces, the ray actually penetrates the wall one square, possibly hitting any phasing monsters in the wall.\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{{$\text{[}$}4{$\text{]}$}}


A directional wand (or spell) may affect not only the first object or creature it hits, but several others behind it. An example of this is healing your pet and a few hostile monsters as well{\mbox{$\text{---}$}}or zapping not only your pet with a wand of speed monster\footnotemark[85]\myhref{85}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Wand_of_speed_monster}, but also a shopkeeper\footnotemark[86]\myhref{86}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Shopkeeper}, angering him.

\section{Using wands}
\label{8}
You may z\footnotemark[118]\myhref{118}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Z}ap or E\footnotemark[119]\myhref{119}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/E}ngrave with a wand. That consumes one charge each time. Covered below,\footnotemark[120]\mylref{120}{25} a\footnotemark[121]\myhref{121}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/A}pply destroys wands.


Zapping a wand with zero charges left usually yields \symbol{34}Nothing happens\symbol{34}. But with a 1 in 121 chance, it instead wrests one last zap and then turns the wand to dust.\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{{$\text{[}$}37{$\text{]}$}} A cancelled wand is shown as \symbol{34}-{}1\symbol{34} charges and will always turn to dust ineffectively if you use it.


Cursed wands have a 1\% chance of exploding if zapped \allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{{$\text{[}$}38{$\text{]}$}} but they otherwise function as normal. Since NetHack 3.6.0\footnotemark[122]\myhref{122}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/NetHack_3.6.0}, cursed wands may also explode when used to engrave.\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{{$\text{[}$}39{$\text{]}$}}


Engraving\footnotemark[123]\myhref{123}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Engraving} with a wand is a good way to work out its identity\footnotemark[124]\myhref{124}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Identification}. Also, engraving with a wand of fire\footnotemark[125]\myhref{125}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Wand_of_fire} or lightning\footnotemark[126]\myhref{126}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Wand_of_lightning} is the only way to make truly permanent writing. E.g. an Elbereth\footnotemark[127]\myhref{127}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Elbereth} that cannot degrade. How many characters you engrave determines how many turns it takes, but it always costs one charge.


You can zap or break with a wand without free hands\footnotemark[128]\myhref{128}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Hand}, e.g. when wielding a cursed two-{}handed weapon; engraving requires hands\footnotemark[129]\myhref{129}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Hand}. However, you can\textquotesingle{}t zap or apply a wand if polymorphed into a form that lacks hands.

Create monster, enlightenment, light, secret door detection and wishing perform their usual effect and will self-{}identify prior to the game giving you the engraving prompt. A wand of lightning will blind you if you engrave with it; you can either cure the blindness promptly, or else abort when prompted for the text you want to engrave and avoid it entirely. This also works for a wand of create monster if you are standing on a functional Elbereth and the monster you generated warrants its use.


Also, be careful concerning the identical message for wands of make invisible, teleportation, and cancellation; you don\textquotesingle{}t want to end up teleporting yourself or cancelling a pet by accident. The safest way to find out the exact identity is using the wand on a non-{}blank scroll or non-{}clear potion you neither need nor care if a monster picks it up (for example, scroll of light\footnotemark[166]\myhref{166}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Scroll_of_light}). If nothing happens, it\textquotesingle{}s make invisible; if the item disappears, it\textquotesingle{}s teleport; if the item blanks/clears (or turns into fruit juice if you\textquotesingle{}re using potion of sickness\footnotemark[167]\myhref{167}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion_of_sickness}), it\textquotesingle{}s cancellation.


One \symbol{34}optimal\symbol{34} method to identify a wand with no message is to line up the corpse of a monster and a container\footnotemark[168]\myhref{168}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Container} in front of a door, with either the door or the container locked and the other unlocked, and zap the wand in that direction. If the corpse is revived, the wand is undead turning; if the contents of the container are revealed, the wand is probing\footnotemark[169]\myhref{169}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Wand_of_probing}, and will be formally identified as such; if the container (or the door) locks, it is locking\footnotemark[170]\myhref{170}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Wand_of_locking}, and will be formally identified as such; and if the door (or the container) unlocks, it is opening\footnotemark[171]\myhref{171}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Wand_of_opening}, and will be formally identified as such. Otherwise, the wand is nothing\footnotemark[172]\myhref{172}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Wand_of_nothing}, and will remain unidentified. If the wand does not prompt you for a direction, it is either stasis\footnotemark[173]\myhref{173}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Wand_of_stasis}, or, in some games, nothing.



\subsection{Identification by zapping}
\label{13}
A good way to determine the identity of a wand that makes engravings vanish{\mbox{$\text{---}$}}at the cost of using a second charge{\mbox{$\text{---}$}}is to do this: line up an item and a monster and zap them. If they both vanish it was teleportation, if the monster vanishes it was make invisible, and if neither vanishes it was cancellation. You can also place a junk\footnotemark[174]\myhref{174}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Junk} scroll on the ground and zap it. If it disappears, the wand is teleportation. If the scroll is blanked, the wand is cancellation. If nothing happens, it\textquotesingle{}s make invisible. This technique doesn\textquotesingle{}t result in an invisible monster.


Many wands will self-{}identify on zapping. A single zap under the right circumstances can identify nearly all of these: stand in a room with a teleportation trap and a door that is closed but unlocked. Drop an object that can be recognizably cancelled (such as a junk scroll) on a diagonal to the door (to prevent dangerous beams from hitting you), and lure a visible undead monster onto the same diagonal line. Zap toward the object, monster, and door. If both object and monster vanish, the wand is teleportation; if the monster vanishes and the object doesn\textquotesingle{}t, it is make invisible; if the scroll is blank, it was cancellation; if the door opens, it was opening; if the door is now locked, it was locking; if the monster flees, it was turn undead. If nothing happens, you can step on the teleportation trap to check if it was stasis. If it doesn\textquotesingle{}t self-{}identify, it is a wand of nothing.


Another way to narrow down the identity of a wand that couldn\textquotesingle{}t be engrave-{}identified is to zap it at yourself. If it doesn\textquotesingle{}t ask for a direction, it\textquotesingle{}s either stasis or nothing; wands of probing and undead turning will always self-{}identify (wand of undead turning will also revive corpses in your inventory, so drop or stash them if you don\textquotesingle{}t want it), and otherwise it\textquotesingle{}s either locking, opening, or nothing.



\subsection{Price-{}identification}
\label{14}
Almost any class of items can be price-{}identified\footnotemark[175]\myhref{175}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Price_identification}, but there are two groups of wands for which this is exceptionally useful. Wands of wishing and wands of death are covered in the price identification\footnotemark[176]\myhref{176}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Price_identification} article; both have base cost 500. In addition, the four wands with base cost 175 are all very useful in the early or middle game. Wands of cold, fire, and lightning provide a powerful ranged attack. Wands of fire and lightning offer an instant method of engraving Elbereth. Freezing water with a wand of cold is one way to cross it or to deal with sea monsters\footnotemark[177]\myhref{177}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Sea_monster} and their dreaded instakill. And wands of sleep are great all the time.
