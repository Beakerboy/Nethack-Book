If the weight of the items you are carrying becomes too much then you become encumbered\footnotemark[4]\myhref{4}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Encumbrance}. Items can be blessed, uncursed or cursed\footnotemark[5]\myhref{5}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/BUC}, which affects their properties. Some have charges, and can be charged, most can be destroyed, some are generated randomly, and some are unique.

Many objects can pile up on top of each other on one square\footnotemark[6]\myhref{6}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Square}, but only the symbol of the top one is shown.
