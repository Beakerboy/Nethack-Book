\chapter{Fungi and Molds}
The \textbf{fungus or mold} is a monster class that appears in \textit{NetHack}, and is represented by the uppercase F glyph (\colorbox{black}{\textcolor{white}{\texttt{F}}}). Fungi and molds are designated internally by the macro \textbf{S\_FUNGUS}.

The monster class contains the following monsters:

\begin{myitemize}
\item{}\colorbox{black}{\textcolor{green!66}{\texttt{F}}}{\mbox{$~$}}lichen
\item{}\colorbox{black}{\textcolor{brown}{\texttt{F}}}{\mbox{$~$}}brown mold
\item{}\colorbox{black}{\textcolor{yellow}{\texttt{F}}}{\mbox{$~$}}yellow mold
\item{}\colorbox{black}{\textcolor{green}{\texttt{F}}}{\mbox{$~$}}green mold
\item{}\colorbox{black}{\textcolor{red}{\texttt{F}}}{\mbox{$~$}}red mold
\item{}\colorbox{black}{\textcolor{purple}{\texttt{F}}}{\mbox{$~$}}shrieker
\item{}\colorbox{black}{\textcolor{violet}{\texttt{F}}}{\mbox{$~$}}violet fungus
\end{myitemize}

\section{Common traits}
All fungi and molds are neutral. They are unbreathing, inediate and mindless monsters that lack limbs, eyes\footnotemark[17]\myhref{17}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Eyes} or a head\footnotemark[18]\myhref{18}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Head}, and do not have any MR score\footnotemark[19]\myhref{19}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Magic_resistance_(monster)}; they all possess poison resistance\footnotemark[20]\myhref{20}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Poison_resistance} except for the lichen and green mold, and cannot be seen via infravision\footnotemark[21]\myhref{21}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Infravision} except for the red mold. A hero\footnotemark[22]\myhref{22}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Hero} that polymorphs\footnotemark[23]\myhref{23}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Polymorph} into a fungus or mold gains immunity to sickness\footnotemark[24]\myhref{24}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Immunity_to_sickness} in addition to the monster\textquotesingle{}s other traits.\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{{$\text{[}$}3{$\text{]}$}} Fungi and mold corpses\footnotemark[25]\myhref{25}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Corpse} and tins\footnotemark[26]\myhref{26}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Tin} are considered vegan\footnotemark[27]\myhref{27}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Vegan} food, and is acceptable for herbivorous\footnotemark[28]\myhref{28}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Herbivorous} and omnivorous\footnotemark[29]\myhref{29}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Omnivorous} monsters.

\subsection{Fungi}
The fungi consist of the lichen, shrieker and violet fungus, which all have the minimum possible speed\footnotemark[30]\myhref{30}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Speed} of 1{\mbox{$\text{---}$}}the lichen and violet fungus use touch attacks, while the shrieker has no attacks at all and simply shrieks intermittently, which can wake sleeping monsters and cause other monsters to generate.

\subsection{Molds}
\label{3}
The molds consist of the brown, yellow, green, and red molds{\mbox{$\text{---}$}}they are all sessile\footnotemark[31]\myhref{31}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Sessile} and only possess a passive attack\footnotemark[32]\myhref{32}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Passive_attack} and intrinsic\footnotemark[33]\myhref{33}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Intrinsic} resistances\footnotemark[34]\myhref{34}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Resistance} that usually correspond to their color:

\begin{myitemize}\item{}Brown molds have a passive cold\footnotemark[35]\myhref{35}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Cold} attack and possess poison and cold resistance\footnotemark[36]\myhref{36}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Cold_resistance}. Eating their tin or corpse can grant either resistance.
\item{}Yellow molds have a passive stun\footnotemark[37]\myhref{37}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Stun} attack and possess poison resistance. Eating their tin or corpse can grant poison resistance, but the corpse is poisonous\footnotemark[38]\myhref{38}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Poisonous} and both it and the tin always cause hallucination\footnotemark[39]\myhref{39}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Hallucination}.
\item{}Green molds have a passive acid\footnotemark[40]\myhref{40}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Acid} attack and possess acid resistance\footnotemark[41]\myhref{41}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Acid_resistance} and stoning resistance\footnotemark[42]\myhref{42}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Stoning_resistance}. Eating their tin or corpse does not grant either resistance, but can abort stoning\footnotemark[43]\myhref{43}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Stoning} (though the acidic\footnotemark[44]\myhref{44}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Acidic} corpse will also deal damage).
\item{}Red molds have a passive fire\footnotemark[45]\myhref{45}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Fire_(element)} attack and possess poison and fire resistance\footnotemark[46]\myhref{46}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Fire_resistance}. Eating their tin or corpse can grant either resistance.
\end{myitemize}

\section{Strategy}
\label{6}
Most fungi and molds are not especially dangerous, unless an early hero is careless in attacking any of the molds: the most troublesome that monsters from this class can become involve situations where either a mold blocks a hero\textquotesingle{}s path, or else a shrieker manages to summon an out-{}of-{}depth purple worm\footnotemark[49]\myhref{49}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Purple_worm} with its shrieking. Otherwise, even the weakest of heroes can typically handle any fungus or mold they come across; any form of ranged attack\footnotemark[50]\myhref{50}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Ranged_attack} will suffice for dealing with the stationary molds in particular, even junk\footnotemark[51]\myhref{51}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Junk} such as rocks\footnotemark[52]\myhref{52}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Rock} or spare orcish daggers\footnotemark[53]\myhref{53}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Orcish_dagger}. Early pets may have trouble with molds due to lacking a ranged attack, and unfortunate ones may end up taking more damage passively than they can successfully deal.


Many fungi and molds have a low chance of granting useful resistances from eating their corpses or tins, making it a good idea for a hero to eat the safer ones, especially if trying to obtain poison resistance in particular; for vegetarian and vegan conduct\footnotemark[54]\myhref{54}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Conduct}, they make up about half of the suitable corpses that heroes can obtain resistances from. Fungi and mold corpses that are safe can also be used to feed herbivorous pets, such as the starting pony\footnotemark[55]\myhref{55}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Pony} of a Knight\footnotemark[56]\myhref{56}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Knight}.

\section{Origin}
\label{8}

A fungus (plural: fungi or funguses) is any type of eukaryotic organism that has chitinous cell walls, but no chlorophyll or plastids, and may may be unicellular or multicellular{\mbox{$\text{---}$}}this includes microorganisms such as yeasts and molds, as well as the more familiar mushrooms. Once believed to be similar to plants, fungi are considered more closely related to animals as they acquire their food by absorbing dissolved molecules, typically by secreting digestive enzymes into their environment. Fungi do not photosynthesize and instead use growth as a means of mobility, though they can also emit spores which may travel through the air or water as a form of reproduction. Fungi often serve as the principal decomposers in ecological systems.


Fungi all occupy single group of related organisms named the Eumycota (\symbol{34}true fungi\symbol{34}) that share a common ancestor, which is strongly supported by molecular phylogenetics. This fungal group is distinct from the structurally similar {\itshape \allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunti.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunti.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\itshape myxomycetes}{$\text{ }$}\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx] (slime molds) and {\itshape \allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunti.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunti.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\itshape oomycetes}{$\text{ }$}\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx] (water molds). Fungi are abundant worldwide, and most are inconspicuous due to their small size and structure along with their presence in soil or on dead matter. They have long been used as a direct source of human food, including but not limited to the following: edible mushrooms and truffles; leavening agents for bread; and the fermentation of various food products, such as wine, beer, and soy sauce. Fungi can break down manufactured materials and buildings, and become significant pathogens of humans and other animals. Losses of crops due to fungal diseases (e.g., rice blast disease) or food spoilage can have a large impact on human food supplies and local economies.


A mold is a colored woolly or furry growth structure of tiny fungi that appears when organic material lies exposed to warm and moist air for a long time, and can also refer to any fungus that creates such growths. Some diseases of animals and humans can be caused by certain molds, e.g. from allergic sensitivity to mold spores, growth of pathogenic molds within the body, or from the effects of ingested or inhaled toxic compounds (mycotoxins) produced by molds. Molds cause biodegradation of natural materials, including food spoilage or damage to property; they also play important roles in biotechnology and food science in the production of various pigments, foods, beverages, antibiotics, pharmaceuticals and enzymes.
