All potions have a weight\footnotemark[18]\myhref{18}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Weight} of 20, and are used by drinking them via the quaff\footnotemark[19]\myhref{19}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Quaff} command; other monsters may be subjected to the effects of some potions by wielding\footnotemark[20]\myhref{20}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Wield} the potion and hitting them with it, or else throwing\footnotemark[21]\myhref{21}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Throw} it at them. It is also possible to dip\footnotemark[22]\myhref{22}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Dip} other items into potions; some potions can be mixed together in this way to produce other potions{\mbox{$\text{---}$}}this is called alchemy\footnotemark[23]\myhref{23}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Alchemy}. 


Potions do not leave behind the empty bottles when consumed; the bottle presumably evaporates or is otherwise disposed of when the potion is drunk.

\section{Identification}
\label{3}
There are many ways to identify potions, without resorting to the obvious methods\footnotemark[80]\myhref{80}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Identification\#Straightforward_identification}. Clear potions are always potions of water, and potions of oil are automatically identified for players starting with an oil lamp\footnotemark[81]\myhref{81}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Oil_lamp}.

\subsection{Applying}
\label{7}
Try to apply\footnotemark[167]\myhref{167}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Apply} every new potion you find. If the potion lights, it will auto-{}identify\footnotemark[168]\myhref{168}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Auto-identify} as a potion of oil. Do not do this with an unpaid potion is owned by a shop, or you will be charged a Yendorian Fuel Tax\footnotemark[169]\myhref{169}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Yendorian_Fuel_Tax}. It is possible to avoid the charge by dropping everything else that can be applied besides the suspected potion, then hit a\footnotemark[170]\myhref{170}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/A}. If the potion is oil, you will be asked what to apply; if it isn\textquotesingle{}t, you will be told that you have nothing that can be applied. This may be a bug.



\subsection{Quaffing}
\label{8}
When you have tried the above methods and all else fails, you can always quaff an unknown potion. However, this will not always identify it, and there are dangers. See potion quaffing effects\footnotemark[171]\myhref{171}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion_quaffing_effects} for a comprehensive list of potion effects when quaffed.


Before you start quaffing, curse-{}test\footnotemark[172]\myhref{172}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Curse-test} the potion, as quaffing a cursed potion is unwise{\mbox{$\text{---}$}}although you will still be able to identify it, you will miss out on some benefits of useful potions and suffer increased damage from harmful ones.


You should wait for your health\footnotemark[173]\myhref{173}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Health} to completely heal before quaffing. That way, should you quaff a potion of healing\footnotemark[174]\myhref{174}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion_of_healing}, extra healing\footnotemark[175]\myhref{175}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion_of_extra_healing}, or full healing\footnotemark[176]\myhref{176}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion_of_full_healing}, your maximum health will increase. In addition, should you quaff a potion of acid\footnotemark[177]\myhref{177}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion_of_acid} or sickness\footnotemark[178]\myhref{178}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion_of_sickness}, it will be much less likely to kill you outright.

\section{Damage}
\label{11}
Fire and cold attacks can cause potions in your main inventory or on the ground to boil or freeze and be destroyed. This does not happen to potions in a container\footnotemark[240]\myhref{240}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Container}, so it is generally advisable to stash potions in one when possible. Potions boiled by fire attacks are vaporized, which can subject you to the effects of the vapors\footnotemark[241]\myhref{241}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Vapors}; potions frozen by cold attacks will not release any vapors.


Potions that are dropped onto very hot ground (e.g. in Gehennom or the Plane of Fire) may heat up and shatter. The odds of potions getting shattered are 30\% if the potion is blessed, or 50\% otherwise. If the potion has been in your inventory at any point, the percentage chance is reduced by double the value of your Luck\footnotemark[242]\myhref{242}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Luck}. The potion of oil\footnotemark[243]\myhref{243}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion_of_oil} is immune from being destroyed by hot ground.\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{{$\text{[}$}4{$\text{]}$}}\}\}



\section{Dilution}
\label{12}
Most potions will become diluted when they get wet\footnotemark[244]\myhref{244}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Wet}, either through immersion in open water, or dipping\footnotemark[245]\myhref{245}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Dip} into open water, a fountain, or another potion. This will not happen when dipping into holy water or unholy water{\mbox{$\text{---}$}}those potions change the beatitude\footnotemark[246]\myhref{246}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Beatitude} of the dipped item without actually getting it wet, and are consumed in the process; potions of water also cannot be diluted.


Diluted potions are listed as such in your inventory, and they will not stack with non-{}diluted potions, but function largely the same as their undiluted counterparts. This can be a minor hassle if your intention is to bless a group of potions with holy water, or if you have no container\footnotemark[247]\myhref{247}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Container} and inventory slots are at a premium. There are also some exceptions, mostly minor:




\begin{myitemize}\item{}A diluted potion of booze will not cure 1HP of damage, as a non-{}diluted one will.
\item{}A diluted potion of see invisible will give somewhat different messages, though the effect is the same.
\item{}A diluted potion of fruit juice gives half the nutrition of a non-{}diluted one.
\end{myitemize}

If a diluted potion is diluted a second time, it will lose any blessed or cursed status, and become an uncursed potion of water. This is often used for making holy water, and provides a reason for most characters to hold on to otherwise useless or harmful potions.


There are some exceptions to the above principles:




\begin{myitemize}\item{}A potion of acid will not dilute, but instead cause an explosion that does damage to the character. If your character dies as a result of this, the cause of death will be listed as \symbol{34}elementary chemistry.\symbol{34}
\item{}A potion of water can not be diluted, which is not surprising, but this means that holy water and unholy water are also immune to dilution, as they are simply blessed or cursed potions of water, and losing beatitude is a consequence of dilution.
\item{}Alchemy will not dilute an already-{}diluted potion, but if the stack of potions being dipped is diluted, only two potions from that stack will be alchemized, so if you convert a bunch of potions of healing into diluted potions of extra healing, you can convert them to diluted potions of full healing only two at a time.
\end{myitemize}

Alchemy\footnotemark[248]\myhref{248}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Alchemy} is a somewhat complicated process which has a few wrinkles of its own; players are advised to study that article for more information before alchemizing, unless they enjoy picking imaginary shards of glass out of their faces.



\section{Thrown potions}
\label{13}
Potions may be wielded\footnotemark[249]\myhref{249}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Wield} or thrown\footnotemark[250]\myhref{250}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Throw} in combat by you or other monsters. A monster has an 80\% chance of taking 1 HP of damage when hit, while you may receive 1 to 2 HP of damage. If you don\textquotesingle{}t see the potion hit the monster, you will get the message \symbol{34}Crash!\symbol{34} A monster hit by a potion is subjected to its effects, but generally to a lesser extent than from quaffing it; however, a thrown potion has a chance of missing the target, in which case it will shatter without affecting them.


When a potion shatters, it is possible for the vapors to affect monsters in adjacent squares, including the player. For this reason, wielding a potion hand-{}to-{}hand can be riskier than throwing it from a distance, although in this case the potion will only shatter when it hits the monster. Circumstances will ultimately dictate which is more preferable for hitting a given monster, e.g. wearing a ring of free action\footnotemark[251]\myhref{251}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Ring_of_free_action} and wielding a potion of paralysis.
