\section{Description}
\label{1}
Sinks have a varying amount of effects depending on how they are interacted with:




\begin{myitemize}\item{}Sinks will cause anything in the air above them to \symbol{34}sink\symbol{34}\footnotemark[31]\myhref{31}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Pun} to the floor, be it thrown weapons\footnotemark[32]\myhref{32}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Weapon} or your levitating\footnotemark[33]\myhref{33}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Levitating} hero\footnotemark[34]\myhref{34}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Hero}. Falling into a sink while levitating will deal up to 25 damage.
\end{myitemize}


\begin{myitemize}\item{}Sitting\footnotemark[35]\myhref{35}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Sit} on a sink will produce the message \symbol{34}Your rump/underside gets wet\symbol{34} (humanoid/not humanoid) with no other effect.\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{{$\text{[}$}1{$\text{]}$}}
\end{myitemize}


\begin{myitemize}\item{}Kicking\footnotemark[36]\myhref{36}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Kick} a sink or quaffing\footnotemark[37]\myhref{37}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Quaff} from it has its own set of effects -{} most of them are negative, though there a few notably beneficial ones.
\end{myitemize}


\begin{myitemize}\item{}Dropping rings\footnotemark[38]\myhref{38}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Ring} into a sink will cause them to be lost down the drain, with a message that enables you to identify the ring.\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{{$\text{[}$}2{$\text{]}$}}
\end{myitemize}


\begin{myitemize}\item{}Digging\footnotemark[39]\myhref{39}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Digging} down on a sink will turn it into a (non-{}magical) fountain\footnotemark[40]\myhref{40}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Fountain} (which can then be dug down onto to produce pools\footnotemark[41]\myhref{41}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Pool} for blanking and diluting\footnotemark[42]\myhref{42}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Wet} if so desired).\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{{$\text{[}$}3{$\text{]}$}}
\end{myitemize}

Per commit 3fac6374\footnotemark[45]\myhref{45}{https://github.com/NetHack/NetHack/commit/3fac63749a399f9fadfecce7ccd6599efdba0866}, it is possible to dip\footnotemark[46]\myhref{46}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Dip} an object into a sink or wash your hands in a sink. For most objects, this merely causes water damage\footnotemark[47]\myhref{47}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Water_damage}; if the object is a potion\footnotemark[48]\myhref{48}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion}, however, you will pour it down the drain to identify it. There are several potential outcomes depending on the potion:




\begin{myitemize}\item{}A potion of polymorph\footnotemark[49]\myhref{49}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion_of_polymorph} will polymorph the sink into a different dungeon feature and automatically identify the potion.
\item{}A potion of oil\footnotemark[50]\myhref{50}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion_of_oil} will leave an oily film and automatically identify the potion.
\item{}A potion of acid\footnotemark[51]\myhref{51}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion_of_acid} will give a message indicating the drain is less clogged unless you are blind or deaf. It will not automatically identify the potion, but if you get the message, you are prompted to call that type of potion.
\item{}A potion of levitation\footnotemark[52]\myhref{52}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion_of_levitation} will cause the sink to back up as if you had kicked it, producing a ring if the sink has not already produced one. You will be prompted to call the potion.
\item{}A potion of object detection\footnotemark[53]\myhref{53}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion_of_object_detection} will cause you to sense a ring lost down the drain if the sink has not already produced one, and prompt you to call the potion.
\item{}Other potions produce effects depending on the effects of their vapors\footnotemark[54]\myhref{54}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Vapor}. Potions that do not leave vapors will not produce any interesting effect, while potions that do will produce the same effect as breathing in the vapors.
\end{myitemize}

\subsection{Identifying rings with a sink}
\label{4}
All rings\footnotemark[71]\myhref{71}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Ring} can be identified by dropping them into sinks.\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{{$\text{[}$}2{$\text{]}$}} The sink will show an effect depending on the type of ring. Rings of searching and slow digestion will never be lost; all other rings will be lost down the drain with 95\% probability.\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{{$\text{[}$}11{$\text{]}$}} Therefore, it is a good idea to test only if you have another ring of the same type as the one being tested, or if you are starving and desperate.


After receiving the message, you will be given the opportunity to name the kind of ring you dropped if you have not already named it. A ring of hunger will also not give you the opportunity if it fails to destroy an item.


Half the messages require you to not be blind\footnotemark[72]\myhref{72}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Blind}, and the ring of hunger requires that there be a (preferably worthless) object already on the sink. \section{Strategy}
\label{5}
Quaffing from sinks is generally a dangerous gamble without much reward. At best you get a ring, which you could already get by kicking it (which is much safer). At worst, you get a potion of sickness or polymorph into something that will break your armor. The chance of something bad is significantly higher than the chance of something good (look at the chart of possibilities).


On the other hand, kicking sinks is fairly safe provided you\textquotesingle{}re past about experience level 5. Foocubi\footnotemark[113]\myhref{113}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Foocubi} pose no significant threat and may be beneficial, and black puddings are slow enough that they won\textquotesingle{}t kill you if you move carefully. It\textquotesingle{}s unwise to kick sinks at low experience levels (1{\mbox{$-$}}3), since foocubi can cause instadeath through level drain.


Remember that if you plan on eating jewelry\footnotemark[114]\myhref{114}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Eating_jewelry}, only metal and wood rings are edible. Since you can\textquotesingle{}t get the intrinsics from non-{}edible rings, you\textquotesingle{}ll only need one to wear if you want that extrinsic; dropping one more down the sink isn\textquotesingle{}t costing you very much.

\begin{myitemize}\item{}First, there is a 1 in (17{\mbox{$~$}}+ Luck) chance of the potion being cursed.

\subsection{xNetHack}
\label{12}
In xNetHack\footnotemark[152]\myhref{152}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/XNetHack}, all sinks generate with 1 random ring buried under them, rings dropped into the sink have an 80\% chance of ending up buried underneath it, and kicking a sink may dislodge one of the rings buried under it. This makes it more likely that rings dropped down the sink can be recovered by kicking that sink again.


Like FIQHack, xNetHack also adds the ring of carrying\footnotemark[153]\myhref{153}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Ring_of_carrying}, but has different messages that allow distinguishing its enchantment, and they won\textquotesingle{}t show up when the player is blind.
