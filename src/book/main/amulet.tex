\section{Generation}
\label{1}
Amulets comprise 1\% of all randomly-{}generated items in the main dungeon, 1\% in containers, and 4\% in Gehennom\footnotemark[23]\myhref{23}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Gehennom}. The second column below is the relative probability of each subtype.


The ones generated 90.5\% cursed are 9\% uncursed and 0.5\% blessed. All others are generated 5\% cursed, 90\% uncursed, 5\% blessed\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{{$\text{[}$}1{$\text{]}$}}.


The Amulet of Yendor is a unique item\footnotemark[24]\myhref{24}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Unique_item} found only in Gehennom\footnotemark[25]\myhref{25}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Gehennom}, with fake counterparts appearing on the Rogue level\footnotemark[26]\myhref{26}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Rogue_level}, in the inventories of certain high-{}level monsters, and in the bones files of people who had a real or fake Amulet themselves, where the real Amulet is replaced with a fake one.



\section{Description}
\label{2}
All amulets have a weight\footnotemark[27]\myhref{27}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Weight} of 20, are made of iron\footnotemark[28]\myhref{28}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Iron}, and have a base price of 150 zorkmids\footnotemark[29]\myhref{29}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Zorkmid}, with the exception of cheap plastic imitations of the Amulet of Yendor\footnotemark[30]\myhref{30}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Cheap_plastic_imitations_of_the_Amulet_of_Yendor} (made of plastic\footnotemark[31]\myhref{31}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Plastic} and worth nothing) and artifact\footnotemark[32]\myhref{32}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Artifact} amulets.


Amulets are put on\footnotemark[33]\myhref{33}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Put_on} around the wearer\textquotesingle{}s neck, and a hero\footnotemark[34]\myhref{34}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Hero} wearing the amulet that isn\textquotesingle{}t a cheap plastic imitation of the Amulet of Yendor\footnotemark[35]\myhref{35}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Cheap_plastic_imitation_of_the_Amulet_of_Yendor} will burn an extra 1 point of nutrition\footnotemark[36]\myhref{36}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Nutrition} every 20 turns.\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{{$\text{[}$}2{$\text{]}$}} Carrying the Amulet of Yendor consumes an additional 1 nutrition per 20 turns, regardless of whether the character is wearing it.
