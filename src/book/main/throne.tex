\section{Strategy}
\label{7}
Given these possibilities, it is safest to take the following precautions before sitting on a throne:




\begin{myitemize}\item{}Drop all your gold, or stash it in a container\footnotemark[114]\myhref{114}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Container}, so it won\textquotesingle{}t be taken away. This should be easy as a throne room normally contains a chest\footnotemark[115]\myhref{115}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Chest} that you can temporarily use to stash your coins.
\item{}Have a unicorn horn\footnotemark[116]\myhref{116}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Unicorn_horn} so that you can undo bad effects such as blindness\footnotemark[117]\myhref{117}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Blindness} and attribute loss\footnotemark[118]\myhref{118}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Attribute}.
\item{}Have positive Luck.
\item{}Be able to deal with several monsters if they appear.
\end{myitemize}

Unicorn horns can no longer restore reduced attributes. Alternatives include potions of restore ability\footnotemark[120]\myhref{120}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion_of_restore_ability}, the restore ability spell\footnotemark[121]\myhref{121}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Spellbook_of_restore_ability}, prayer\footnotemark[122]\myhref{122}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Prayer}, or finding a way to exercise or increase that attribute again.
In addition, it can be helpful to do the following:




\begin{myitemize}\item{}Heal to within 5 points of maximum.
\item{}Take out any items you would like identified.
\item{}Stow any items you would prefer unidentified.
\item{}Decide what you would like to wish for.
\item{}Decide which single species you would like to genocide.
\item{}Put on a ring of teleport control\footnotemark[123]\myhref{123}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Ring_of_teleport_control} if you have one available and don\textquotesingle{}t otherwise have teleport control\footnotemark[124]\myhref{124}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Teleport_control}.
\end{myitemize}

If the throne causes you to teleport and you have teleport control\footnotemark[125]\myhref{125}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Teleport_control}, you should choose to teleport to a different square rather than remain on the throne. This eliminates the chance that the throne will disappear from that use.


Confused looting (with gold and without a chest) is fairly safe and cheap. It can be a great source\footnotemark[126]\myhref{126}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Farming\#Throne_farming} of dragons and things that they provide (scales, corpses (resistances), loot, pets).

\section{Encyclopedia entry}
\label{12}

Methought I saw the footsteps of a throne$\text{ }$\newline{}

Which mists and vapours from mine eyes did shroud-{}-{}$\text{ }$\newline{}

Nor view of who might sit thereon allowed;$\text{ }$\newline{}

But all the steps and ground about were strown$\text{ }$\newline{}

With sights the ruefullest that flesh and bone$\text{ }$\newline{}

Ever put on; a miserable crowd,$\text{ }$\newline{}

Sick, hale, old, young, who cried before that cloud,$\text{ }$\newline{}

\symbol{34}Thou art our king, O Death! to thee we groan.\symbol{34}$\text{ }$\newline{}


Those steps I clomb; the mists before me gave$\text{ }$\newline{}

Smooth way; and I beheld the face of one$\text{ }$\newline{}

Sleeping alone within a mossy cave,$\text{ }$\newline{}

With her face up to heaven; that seemed to have$\text{ }$\newline{}

Pleasing remembrance of a thought foregone;$\text{ }$\newline{}

A lovely Beauty in a summer grave!$\text{ }$\newline{}




{$\text{[}$} Sonnet, by William Wordsworth {$\text{]}$}
