\section{Generation}
\label{1}
Rings comprise 3\% of all randomly-{}generated items in the main dungeon, 5\% each in containers\footnotemark[19]\myhref{19}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Container} and on the Rogue level\footnotemark[20]\myhref{20}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Rogue_level}, and 8\% in Gehennom\footnotemark[21]\myhref{21}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Gehennom}. All types of rings are equally likely to be generated: this means a 0.179\% chance each in containers and on the Rogue level, a 0.286\% chance in Gehennom, and a 0.107\% chance elsewhere.

\section{Description}
\label{2}
Rings are worn by using P\footnotemark[27]\myhref{27}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/P} to put them on, and removed using R\footnotemark[28]\myhref{28}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/R}; one ring can be worn on each hand\footnotemark[29]\myhref{29}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Hand}. While there is no difference between the dominant or nondominant hands in terms of the ring\textquotesingle{}s effects, a cursed\footnotemark[30]\myhref{30}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Cursed} wielded weapon\footnotemark[31]\myhref{31}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Weapon} may prevent access to the finger on the dominant hand.

\subsection{Ring hunger}
\label{3}
Furthermore, with the exception of the meat ring\footnotemark[37]\myhref{37}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Meat_ring} and chargeable rings at +0 charge, worn rings have a 5\% chance to cause losing an additional nutrition point every turn. This is cumulative{\mbox{$\text{---}$}}a player wearing two rings will lose 2 additional hunger points every 20 turns on average. This sense of \symbol{34}ring hunger\symbol{34} is distinct from the additional hunger-{}causing effects of rings of regeneration\footnotemark[38]\myhref{38}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Ring_of_regeneration}, conflict\footnotemark[39]\myhref{39}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Ring_of_conflict}, and hunger\footnotemark[40]\myhref{40}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Ring_of_hunger}, all of which cause a nutrition point to be lost every other turn. The ring of slow digestion\footnotemark[41]\myhref{41}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Ring_of_slow_digestion} prevents ordinary nutrition consumption, but still causes ring hunger. A hero with slow digestion from a non-{}ring source (i.e. white dragon scales\footnotemark[42]\myhref{42}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/White_dragon_scales} or white dragon scale mail\footnotemark[43]\myhref{43}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/White_dragon_scale_mail}) will still incur ring hunger, even if they are not wearing any rings or amulet.\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{{$\text{[}$}4{$\text{]}$}}


A worn +0 ring of protection\footnotemark[44]\myhref{44}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Ring_of_protection} causes ring hunger if it is the hero\textquotesingle{}s only source of extrinsic protection{\mbox{$\text{---}$}}if the hero is wearing two rings of protection and the one on their left hand is +0, ring hunger applies to that left-{}hand ring even if the right hand\textquotesingle{}s ring is charged.\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{{$\text{[}$}5{$\text{]}$}}


For a full list of hunger causes and amounts, see the articles on nutrition\footnotemark[45]\myhref{45}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Nutrition} and foodless\footnotemark[46]\myhref{46}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Foodless}.
