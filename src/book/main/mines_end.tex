\section{Mines' End}
{\bfseries \allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunbx.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunbx.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\bfseries Mines\textquotesingle{} End}{$\text{ }$}\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx] lies at the bottom of the Gnomish Mines\footnotemark[10]\myhref{10}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Gnomish_Mines}. It will be either the 8th or 9th level of that branch and will therefore occur somewhere between Dlvl10 and Dlvl13. There are three versions of Mines\textquotesingle{} End; each of which is guaranteed to contain a luckstone\footnotemark[11]\myhref{11}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Luckstone}.


Mines\textquotesingle{} End, being the lowest level of the Gnomish Mines, has an undiggable\footnotemark[12]\myhref{12}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Undiggable} floor. Therefore, zapping a wand of digging\footnotemark[13]\myhref{13}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Wand_of_digging} downwards will produce a pit instead of a hole.

This level contains seven hidden places, marked on the map below. Six of them are behind secret doors\footnotemark[20]\myhref{20}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Secret_door}, and the seventh is behind a narrow choke point\footnotemark[21]\myhref{21}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Movement_tactics\#Passing_small_gaps}.

\item{}nothing
\end{myitemize}

In addition, there are two random tools, seven random gems, three other random objects, and six random traps placed throughout the level. The rubies, emeralds, and diamonds can be identified because they are hard gems, while the amethysts can be identified because they are always grouped with the diamond and green glass.  In addition, the luckstone and loadstone can be identified by the order of the red gem piles: if the worthless glass is in the middle of the pile, then the gray stone is a luckstone, otherwise it is a loadstone.  The gray stones that the mimics are imitating can also be identified by giving a temporary type-{}name\footnotemark[48]\myhref{48}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Type-name} to the \symbol{34}object\symbol{34} on the floor (before you disturb the mimic), examining the other gems in the pile, then naming the imitated gray stone with the appropriate identification through the discoveries list.  This can allow you to informally identify all of the gray stones even if you have never encountered them.


All walls are undiggable.

\section{The Gnome King\textquotesingle{}s Wine Cellar}

This version contains a room behind two secret doors (here, marked S) in the northwest, containing three piles of potions, each pile containing two guaranteed potions of booze\footnotemark[63]\myhref{63}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion_of_booze} (as the name would suggest), and one other kind of potion. For the southernmost pile, the other potion is guaranteed to be a potion of object detection\footnotemark[64]\myhref{64}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion_of_object_detection}; the other potions in the two northern piles are random.


Scattered throughout the level, there are two random tools, seven random gems, three other random objects, and six random traps. The luckstone\footnotemark[65]\myhref{65}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Luckstone} is at the chamber in the northeast; the blank tiles are rock that needs to be dug out to reach this chamber. The chamber also contains one ruby\footnotemark[66]\myhref{66}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Ruby} and one emerald\footnotemark[67]\myhref{67}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Emerald}{\mbox{$~$}}(SW), two diamonds\footnotemark[68]\myhref{68}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Diamond}{\mbox{$~$}}(NW), two emeralds\footnotemark[69]\myhref{69}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Emerald}{\mbox{$~$}}(NE), two amethysts\footnotemark[70]\myhref{70}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Amethyst}, one ruby\footnotemark[71]\myhref{71}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Ruby} and one luckstone{\mbox{$~$}}(SE), as well as a few random gems. To get to the hidden gem cache, you can teleport in or dig with a pickaxe. As the spell/wand of digging only digs one tile here, you\textquotesingle{}ll need more charges than one wand can hold, so they make a poor choice.

This level is unmappable and contains a randomly-{}generated maze. The level walls, the large chambers, and the possible locations of the luckstone\footnotemark[107]\myhref{107}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Luckstone} are fixed. There are three possible locations for the luckstone. Of the two locations that do not contain the luckstone, one will have nothing and the other a flint stone. The luckstone and the flint stone lie on level teleporters\footnotemark[108]\myhref{108}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Level_teleporter}. The rest of the level contains two diamonds, three emeralds, two rubies, two amethysts, seven other random gems, five random scrolls, four random spellbooks, three other random objects, and seven more random traps (so that the chance of at least one polytrap\footnotemark[109]\myhref{109}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Polytrap} is  31\%).\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{{$\text{[}$}2{$\text{]}$}}


There are two random V\footnotemark[110]\myhref{110}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/V} in the level but they will appear in their shape-{}changed d\footnotemark[111]\myhref{111}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/D} and B\footnotemark[112]\myhref{112}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/B} forms unless you defeat them in battle. \subsection{Strategy}
\label{5}
The spellbooks and scrolls make this the most desirable level for spellcasters, but it is also the most difficult, because the starting room often contains several powerful monsters in a small space.


There are a couple of different ways that you can get the luckstone off the level teleporter\footnotemark[122]\myhref{122}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Level_teleporter} traps:




\begin{myitemize}\item{}Step onto the trap directly.

\begin{myitemize}\item{}If you have magic resistance\footnotemark[123]\myhref{123}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Magic_resistance}, nothing will happen and you can pick up the stone.
\item{}If you have teleport control\footnotemark[124]\myhref{124}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Teleport_control}, you can choose to teleport to the current level and the trap will be removed.
\item{}Otherwise, you are teleported to a random level above and will have to make your way back -{} the trap will be removed after this happens.
\end{myitemize}

\item{}Zap the stone with a wand of teleportation\footnotemark[125]\myhref{125}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Wand_of_teleportation}, which will teleport it to a random location on the level.
\end{myitemize}

\subsection{SLASH\textquotesingle{}EM}
\label{8}
SLASH\textquotesingle{}EM contains an additional level to the Gnomish Mines which is reached by a down staircase in the now misnamed Mines\textquotesingle{} End. The Mine King level\footnotemark[201]\myhref{201}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Mine_king_level} can also be dug to through the floor of Mines\textquotesingle{} End. This is important mainly not as an escape, but as a warning to be alert for trapdoors which can drop you into a situation you are not prepared for (the new final level of the mines can be difficult for the unprepared). The down staircase will be located on one of the possible luckstone\footnotemark[202]\myhref{202}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Luckstone} locations for the \symbol{34}Mimic of the Mines\symbol{34} and the \symbol{34}Catacombs\symbol{34} variants and immediately next to the gem cache in the \symbol{34}Gnome King\textquotesingle{}s Wine Cellar\symbol{34} variant.
