\section{Dipping}
\label{5}
If you \#dip\footnotemark[62]\myhref{62}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Dip} a single ordinary long sword in a fountain, are at least experience level 5, and Excalibur\footnotemark[63]\myhref{63}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Excalibur} doesn\textquotesingle{}t already exist in your game, then there is a \allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{1}⁄\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsubscript{6} chance of something special happening to your long sword. If you are lawful, the long sword is converted into the blessed, uneroded, erodeproof Excalibur, and you exercise your wisdom. If you are neutral or chaotic, your long sword is cursed, loses its rustproofing, and may lose an enchantment, and you abuse your wisdom. In either case, the fountain then disappears. \section{Strategy}
\label{10}
\subsection{Fountain quaffing}
\label{11}
Fountain quaffing is the specific practice of immediately quaffing from every fountain you find, in the hope of releasing a water demon\footnotemark[90]\myhref{90}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Water_demon} and getting an early wish\footnotemark[91]\myhref{91}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Wish}. In a lesser form, most inexperienced players will unintentionally engage in this behavior; this is unfortunate as most fountain quaffers die after a few hundred turns, but those with experience don\textquotesingle{}t mind because they can just start again. This is a form of metagaming\footnotemark[92]\myhref{92}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Metagaming} considered fairly low. It is the reason why water moccasins\footnotemark[93]\myhref{93}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Water_moccasin} rank so highly on the list of top deaths\footnotemark[94]\myhref{94}{https://alt.org/nethack/topdeaths.html} on nethack.alt.org\footnotemark[95]\myhref{95}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Nethack.alt.org}, and perhaps the explanation why quaffing from fountains in general is a leading cause of death there (along with deaths to Team Ant\footnotemark[96]\myhref{96}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Ant}).  Wizards who start with a scroll of taming, a tooled horn\footnotemark[97]\myhref{97}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Tooled_horn} or leather drum\footnotemark[98]\myhref{98}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Leather_drum}, or a spellbook of charm monster\footnotemark[99]\myhref{99}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Spellbook_of_charm_monster} are slightly safer in this practice.



\subsection{Magic fountain hunting}
\label{12}
Some players prefer to go hunting for magic fountains for their blessed gain ability effect. This is typically an early-{}midgame strategy performed after the player is confident they have 4+ Luck and can deal with any of the negative fountain effects they might encounter from a normal fountain. It is a good idea to protect your inventory from curses with a sack. The approach is simple: repeatedly quaff from each randomly-{}placed fountain until something other than \symbol{34}The cool draught refreshes you.\symbol{34} happens. If the fountain is magic, the something else will be the magic effect; if not, the random fountain effect will indicate a non-{}magic fountain -{} if you should happen to get a wish in the process, so much the better. Don\textquotesingle{}t bother quaffing in Minetown\footnotemark[100]\myhref{100}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Minetown}, Mines\textquotesingle{} End\footnotemark[101]\myhref{101}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Mines\%27_End}, the center of the Oracle room\footnotemark[102]\myhref{102}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Oracle_(level)}, the Big Room\footnotemark[103]\myhref{103}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Big_Room}, the Castle\footnotemark[104]\myhref{104}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Castle}, or Juiblex\textquotesingle{}s swamp\footnotemark[105]\myhref{105}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Juiblex\%27s_swamp}{\mbox{$\text{---}$}}those are never magic. 


You don\textquotesingle{}t lose any expected wishes by doing so, but because the consequences of quaffing are worse than those of dipping, once you know a fountain is non-{}magical, if you want to try to get wishes from it it is best to do so by dipping objects not quaffing them.


The expected number of items cursed per gain ability effect is \allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{i}⁄\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsubscript{20}, where i is the number of items you have in your inventory while quaffing. If you drop items (perhaps because you have no sack) it\textquotesingle{}s worth noting that quaffing can put pools on nearby map squares, but not on the square of the fountain itself. \allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{{$\text{[}$}6{$\text{]}$}} You can drop items you really don\textquotesingle{}t want cursed, such as your luckstone\footnotemark[106]\myhref{106}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Luckstone}, unicorn horn\footnotemark[107]\myhref{107}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Unicorn_horn}, or bag of holding\footnotemark[108]\myhref{108}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Bag_of_holding}, right on the fountain or in a nearby doorway while you quaff, to keep them from ending up at the bottom of a pool.



\subsection{Dipping for Excalibur}
\label{13}
An obvious strategy for lawful\footnotemark[109]\myhref{109}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Lawful} characters, especially valkyries\footnotemark[110]\myhref{110}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Valkyrie} and knights\footnotemark[111]\myhref{111}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Knight} who start the game with a longsword. As soon as they reach level 5, they start dipping their longsword into every (non-{}Minetown\footnotemark[112]\myhref{112}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Minetown}) fountain they can find until they receive Excalibur\footnotemark[113]\myhref{113}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Excalibur}. At level 5, these combat-{}strong characters may even stand a decent chance against the more dangerous fountain effects, and the hoped-{}for eventual conversion of the thoroughly rusted, possibly now cursed longsword fixes these issues.



\subsection{Making holy water}
\label{14}
A potion of holy water\footnotemark[114]\myhref{114}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion_of_holy_water} (blessed potion of water) is useful for uncursing and blessing items as well as curing lycanthropy and illness. Potions could be converted into potions of water by dipping into a fountain twice, and then blessed on an altar\footnotemark[115]\myhref{115}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Altar} as described in the holy water article. Unfortunately, the fountain has a chance of drying up before you have converted all of your potions. The same applies to blanking scrolls and spellbooks.


The solution is to stand on a fountain and dig down. The fountain will be destroyed with a large chance of making several pools\footnotemark[116]\myhref{116}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Moat} of water. Potions can then be dipped in them without using up your water supply. Unburden yourself, carry only items you want blanked, rusted or converted to potions of water, and jump into the pool. You will crawl out (and get a scary message).  Alternatively, water walking boots\footnotemark[117]\myhref{117}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Water_walking_boots} or flying\footnotemark[118]\myhref{118}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Flying} let you \#dip\footnotemark[119]\myhref{119}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Dip} while standing on the water without getting yourself wet. Beware that potions of acid might explode, although not always. If you jump into pools, they will never deal damage, identify, or convert to water, but they might survive.\allowbreak{}\setmainfont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmunrm,BoldFont=cmunbx,ItalicFont=cmunti,BoldItalicFont=cmunbi]\setmonofont{cmunrm.ttf}[RawFeature={fallback=myfallback},Path=/usr/share/fonts/truetype/cmu/,UprightFont=cmuntt,BoldFont=cmuntb,ItalicFont=cmunit,BoldItalicFont=cmuntx]\textsuperscript{{$\text{[}$}7{$\text{]}$}}



\subsection{Removing curses}
\label{15}
Once your character becomes powerful enough to withstand the bad fountain results (water demons, nymphs, and mocassins are the main ones to watch out for), dipping items (especially items that take no water damage) into fountains is one of the more readily-{}available means to uncurse them.  This is most useful for cursed unicorn horns\footnotemark[120]\myhref{120}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Unicorn_horn} found in bones, and magic lamps\footnotemark[121]\myhref{121}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Magic_lamp} that happen to generate cursed (uncursing them via fountain means you need less holy water to eventually bless them). It can also be useful for cursed equipment; for instance levitation boots\footnotemark[122]\myhref{122}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Levitation_boots} often generate cursed, provide an important extrinsic, and are usable even if rotted or burnt. (However, levitation boots cannot be dipped into a fountain while worn, so you need to uncurse them in advance.) You should not rely on being able to uncurse items this way, as the fountain will frequently dry up first, but it is frequently worth trying before resorting to consumables like scrolls of remove curse\footnotemark[123]\myhref{123}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Scroll_of_remove_curse} or potions of holy water\footnotemark[124]\myhref{124}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion_of_holy_water}.



\subsection{Obtaining curses}
\label{16}
Some items are more useful cursed\footnotemark[125]\myhref{125}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Unholy_water\#Possible_uses}. If you don\textquotesingle{}t find any bones\footnotemark[126]\myhref{126}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Bones} piles, the next safest conduct\footnotemark[127]\myhref{127}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Conduct}-{}proof way to make unholy water\footnotemark[128]\myhref{128}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Unholy_water} is to dip a potion of water\footnotemark[129]\myhref{129}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion_of_water} into fountains until it becomes cursed. Prepare for the hazards! This works best if the game shows the potion\textquotesingle{}s BUC\footnotemark[130]\myhref{130}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/BUC} status because you won\textquotesingle{}t get a message, but you can also keep testing if the potion still stacks\footnotemark[131]\myhref{131}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Stack} with known-{}uncursed water. Once you have it, unholy water can be replicated just like holy water.


If you want to kill e.g. 120 skeletons\footnotemark[132]\myhref{132}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Skeleton}, you might want to curse (and polymorph) many unicorn horns. You can curse a very large number of items at once by quaffing. Carry only those items plus the means to defend yourself against snakes, water demons, nymphs. Quaff from fountains until you get the message \symbol{34}This water\textquotesingle{}s no good!\symbol{34} If you run out of fountains, use spellcasting monsters. You will likely need to uncurse your armor when you are finished.


Of course, reading a blessed scroll of remove curse\footnotemark[133]\myhref{133}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Scroll_of_remove_curse} while confused provides a much safer way to make unholy water.



\subsection{Small swamps}
\label{17}
In very small rooms, a swamp\footnotemark[134]\myhref{134}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Swamp} may generate with just one water pool\footnotemark[135]\myhref{135}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Pool}, easily mistaken for a fountain. Be careful not to accidentally fall into pools.
