\section{Generation}
\label{2}
Scrolls constitute 16\% of all randomly-{}generated items in the main dungeon, 18\% in containers, 22\% on the Rogue level, and 1\% in Gehennom. Scrolls appear 1/8 cursed, 3/4 uncursed, and 1/8 blessed.


On top of random generation, scrolls can be found in book shops\footnotemark[68]\myhref{68}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Shops}, in bones\footnotemark[69]\myhref{69}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Bones} files, and amongst the possessions of recently deceased monsters.

The Ink column gives the greatest number of magic marker\footnotemark[71]\myhref{71}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Magic_marker} charges required to write a scroll. The actual number of charges used will be a random amount from Ink/2 to Ink-{}1; if your marker has fewer than Ink/2 charges, you will be unable to attempt to write the scroll.


Wizards have a base 1/3 chance of writing an unknown scroll, and all other classes have a base 1/15 chance; your chance generally improves with higher Luck\footnotemark[72]\myhref{72}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Luck}, to a maximum of about 98\% for Wizards and 32\% for other classes. You can only write scrolls on blank paper, which can be made by getting any scroll wet (except mail) or cancelling any scroll (except mail). Failure to write the scroll will cause the blank scroll to be used up. You can select non-{}blank scrolls to write on, but this will abuse your wisdom and you will be told, \symbol{34}That scroll is not blank!\symbol{34} You need hands to write a scroll.



\section{Reading scrolls}
\label{4}
All scrolls take one turn to read and are destroyed if successfully read, except for the scroll of blank paper\footnotemark[73]\myhref{73}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Scroll_of_blank_paper} which has no effect.


To read a scroll, you must either be unblind or know the appearance of the scroll. When a scroll is read, it will be used up and disappear (except blank paper). The effects depend on the blessing of the scroll. Non-{}cursed means either blessed or uncursed. If you are confused while reading the scroll, \symbol{34}you mispronounce the magic words\symbol{34} and may obtain a different effect (this applies to confused monsters reading scrolls, too). Some scrolls have a different message for \symbol{34}beginners\symbol{34}; a character is considered a beginner until accumulating 2000 score points (1000 for wizards\footnotemark[74]\myhref{74}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Wizard}). See the individual scroll articles for details on each scroll.

\subsection{Scare monster}
\label{6}
There are several ways to indirectly identify the scroll of scare monster:




\begin{myitemize}\item{}You stand on the scroll and adjacent monsters turn to flee (including pets {\mbox{$\&$}} shopkeepers)
\item{}You pick up the scroll and it turns to dust
\item{}You are a Priest(ess)\footnotemark[75]\myhref{75}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Priest}, and a scroll changes its BUC\footnotemark[76]\myhref{76}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/BUC} when you pick it up
\item{}You find the scroll under the Sokoban\footnotemark[77]\myhref{77}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Sokoban} prize or the chest with the wand of wishing at the Castle\footnotemark[78]\myhref{78}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Castle}
\end{myitemize}

\subsection{Monster use}
\label{8}
Monsters can be generated with scrolls of teleportation, earth, and create monster. If you observe them reading said scrolls, that identifies the scroll to you.



\subsection{Position}
\label{9}
Scrolls of earth can be identified by the guaranteed ones in the first level of Sokoban\footnotemark[96]\myhref{96}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Sokoban}. Scrolls of teleportation are the only ones generated inside closets\footnotemark[97]\myhref{97}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Closet}.  Scrolls of scare monster are placed under the Sokoban prize and the chest with the wand of wishing at the Castle\footnotemark[98]\myhref{98}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Castle}.



\subsection{Reading}
\label{10}
Many veteran players will not read an unidentified scroll unless they have a pretty good guess what it could be. Nonetheless, if you are desperate and you have a large variety of price-{}identified, pet-{}tested scrolls in your inventory, you may try to identify them by reading them.



\subsubsection{Precautions}
\label{11}
You should not try to read-{}identify scrolls if the game considers your character a beginner\footnotemark[99]\myhref{99}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Beginner}. If your character is a beginner, then several scrolls have a possibility of giving the message \symbol{34}You have a strange feeling for a moment, then it passes.\symbol{34} If your character is not a beginner, then it will be possible to distinguish all scrolls by the messages and effects obtained by reading them.


BUC-{}testing scrolls before reading them is important: some scrolls are much more deadly when cursed.


Some scrolls are less dangerous if read while confused. However, the scroll of genocide\footnotemark[100]\myhref{100}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Scroll_of_genocide} can kill you instantly if read while confused, so read-{}identification while confused is not universally helpful. Don\textquotesingle{}t be blind or hallucinating.


Beware of the scroll of amnesia. There is no defense against the possibility of losing up to 25\% of your discoveries.

\section{Strategy}
\label{16}
Scrolls, along with potions\footnotemark[108]\myhref{108}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Potion} and comestibles\footnotemark[109]\myhref{109}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Comestible}, are one of the main types of consumable items in the game. The fact that they can only be used once tends to encourage hoarding; if you notice yourself frequently dying with large numbers of unused scrolls, consider using them next time!


For scroll-{}specific strategy tips, see the individual articles.
