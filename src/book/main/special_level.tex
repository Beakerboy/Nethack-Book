Usually some parts of these levels are not randomly generated. The special levels are made with a special programming language\footnotemark[1]\myhref{1}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Des-file_format}, which are then compiled into .lev -{}files by lev\_comp\footnotemark[2]\myhref{2}{https://nethackwiki.com/wiki/Lev_comp}. NetHack loads the .lev-{}file when needed and creates the special level based on the instructions in it.
